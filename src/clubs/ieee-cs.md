IEEE Computer Society (IEEE CS) at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Bengaluru, is the premier student community dedicated to advancing computer science, software engineering, and emerging technologies.

The chapter's mission is to bridge the gap between academic learning and practical, real-world engineering. We provide a platform for students to collaborate, innovate, and develop strong technical foundations through hands-on experiences and peer-to-peer mentorship.

What We Do

- Hands-on Workshops & Bootcamps: Deep dives into full-stack web development, machine learning, systems programming, and competitive problem-solving.
- Hackathons & Coding Sprints: Fast-paced challenges that give students opportunities to build production-grade solutions and collaborate across domains.
- Open Source & Research Guidance: Mentorship tracks focused on open-source contributions, developer tooling's, and technical research paper writing.
- Tech Talks & Networking: Sessions connecting students with alumni, industry professionals, and IEEE resources worldwide.
