JIDO is the premier Industrial Automation Club at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Bengaluru, dedicated to fostering innovation, technical expertise, and hands-on learning in the field of industrial automation.

Our mission is to empower students with practical experience in emerging automation technologies while bridging the gap between academic knowledge and real-world industrial applications. Through workshops, hackathons, industrial visits, technical projects, and interactions with industry experts, JIDO provides members with opportunities to explore areas such as robotics, control systems, automation, and smart industrial technologies.

JIDO aims to build a community of passionate innovators who collaborate, experiment, and develop solutions to real-world engineering challenges, while preparing students for the rapidly evolving landscape of Industry 4.0 and industrial automation.
