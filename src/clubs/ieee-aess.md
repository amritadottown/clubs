The IEEE Aerospace and Electronic Systems Society (AESS) is a technical community focused on aerospace, avionics, electronics, embedded systems, robotics, sensing, navigation, control, and related technologies.

The club provides students with opportunities to explore emerging technologies through technical sessions, workshops, projects, competitions, and collaborative activities, while connecting them with the broader IEEE AESS community.
