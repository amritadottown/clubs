Narthana - The Dance Club is the heart of rhythmic expression and creative movement on campus, bringing together students who share a deep passion for dance. Whether you are a seasoned performer or stepping onto the floor for the very first time, the club provides a welcoming, high-energy environment to refine your technique, explore new styles, and express yourself freely through movement.

From training sessions and interactive workshops in classical, contemporary, and fusion genres to high-impact performances at major cultural events, Narthana offers every member the opportunity to take center stage. Beyond the choreography, it is a close-knit creative community where dancers collaborate, build confidence, and forge lasting friendships through a shared love of performance.

Guiding the club's artistic vision and operations is a dedicated leadership team mentored by Faculty Advisor Dr. Shashi Kumar M. E. The office bearer board is led by President Ashrita Josyula, alongside Vice Presidents Ameeshi Pandey and Ishani K. Shreekumar, and Treasurer Siddharth Anil Venkiteswaran. Together, they ensure the club continues to thrive as a vibrant hub for student talent.
