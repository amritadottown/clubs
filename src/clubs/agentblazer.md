**Salesforce × Agentblazer Club**

*Learn. Build. Innovate. Become an Agentblazer.*

The Agentblazer Club at Amrita, in collaboration with Salesforce, is a student-driven community for those who want to go beyond textbooks and experience the intersection of AI, Data, Automation, and Business.

**What We Do**

Our club bridges the gap between academic theory and industry implementation. We guide members from the fundamentals of Salesforce and CRM to cutting-edge enterprise technologies:

- **Agentforce & AI Agents** — Building autonomous, intelligent workflows.
- **Data Cloud** — Unifying real-time data for actionable insights.
- **Automation & Low-Code** — Streamlining complex business operations.
- **Intelligent Solutions** — Solving real-world problems with hands-on projects.

We don't stop at learning concepts — you will build, participate in workshops, solve practical business challenges, and transform ideas into working solutions.

**Who Belongs Here?**

Whether you are a coder, a problem solver, a designer, a business enthusiast, or simply curious about the future of AI, there is a place for you here.

- **Developers:** Build robust apps, integrations, and logic.
- **Business Analysts:** Design end-to-end user journeys and process workflows.
- **Designers & Creators:** Craft seamless UI/UX for modern enterprise platforms.
- **Innovators:** Experiment with AI agents and automation tools.

**What You Get Out of It**

Through hands-on sessions, hackathons, team projects, peer mentorship, and industry-focused activities, we help students build skills that matter beyond the classroom:

1. **Technical Expertise:** Hands-on experience with market-leading software.
2. **Real-World Experience:** Projects you can showcase in your portfolio and resume.
3. **Professional Network:** Collaboration with peers, mentors, and industry experts.
4. **Leadership & Confidence:** Skills to lead projects, manage teams, and build solutions from scratch.

This isn't just another college club. **It's where you learn the technology shaping tomorrow, build with it today, and become ready to lead what comes next.**
