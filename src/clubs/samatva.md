Samatva is dedicated to fostering physical, mental, and emotional well-being through yoga, mindfulness, and holistic practices. We aim to create a space where students can discover balance, build self-awareness, and develop healthier habits through engaging and meaningful experiences.

At the heart of Samatva lies the belief that true well-being comes from harmony within. Through yoga asanas, breathing practices, meditation, and mindfulness, we encourage individuals to connect with themselves, find inner balance, and approach everyday life with greater awareness and calmness.

We believe wellness should be accessible, engaging, and enjoyable. Alongside regular yoga and mindfulness practices, we conduct interactive workshops, activities, challenges, and community initiatives that make holistic well-being a part of student life.

Samatva is more than a yoga club — it is a community that encourages balance, awareness, and growth, one practice at a time.
