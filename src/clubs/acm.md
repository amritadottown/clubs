We're ACM, the student chapter of the Association for Computing Machinery at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Bengaluru.

We're a student community focused on computing, technology, and innovation. From algorithms and competitive programming to AI, cybersecurity, software engineering, and research, we provide students with opportunities to explore different areas of computing and develop practical skills.

Through workshops, hackathons, coding competitions, speaker sessions, projects, and technical discussions, we aim to bridge the gap between classroom learning and real-world technology. We encourage students to learn by doing, collaborate with others, and develop strong technical and problem-solving skills.

Whether you're just getting started or already exploring advanced areas of computing, ACM ASEB is a space to learn, build, collaborate, and grow with a community of students passionate about technology.
